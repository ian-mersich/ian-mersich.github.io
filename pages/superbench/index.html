Figure Tool

Build editable scientific figures and presentations with vector drawing tools, page-based workflows, and released scientific assets.

Portable project files keep authored content available for later editing.

Open Figure Tool

miRNA Predictor

Estimate the abundance of 465 mature microRNAs from supported RNA-seq expression tables. Review gene coverage, inspect sample-level predictions and miRNA-specific study comparisons, and download the results.

Prediction runs in your browser; expression files are not sent to an inference server. Estimates support hypothesis generation and do not replace direct small-RNA measurements.
