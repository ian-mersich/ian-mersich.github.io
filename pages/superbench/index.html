RNA-seq expression & miRNA prediction

miRNA Predictor

Estimate the abundance of 465 mature miRNAs from supported RNA-seq expression tables. Review input coverage, inspect predictions and study comparisons, and download results.

Expression files stay on your device. Estimates support hypothesis generation and do not replace direct mature-miRNA measurement.
