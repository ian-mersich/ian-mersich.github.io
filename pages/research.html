Research

Manuscript in preparation · 2026

From RNA-seq to mature microRNAs

Can gene-expression profiles help estimate mature microRNA abundance when small-RNA measurements are unavailable?

I developed and compared models using matched gene-expression and small-RNA profiles. The study tests which RNA-seq inputs are informative, compares regression and neural approaches, and asks when biological context improves prediction across microRNAs, cancer types, and tumor–normal comparisons.

The released browser app provides the separately fitted P1 model for estimating 465 mature microRNAs from supported RNA-seq expression tables. These estimates support hypothesis generation and do not replace direct mature-miRNA measurements. The manuscript is in preparation; a preprint is not yet available.

2026 · iScience

Reprogramming broad drug resistance

Can shared molecular features of broadly drug-resistant cancer cells identify compounds that improve treatment response?

I integrated PRISM drug-response data with transcriptomic, mutation, and metabolomic profiles to characterize resistance-associated programs. We then used gene-expression perturbation resources to prioritize compounds predicted to counter those programs.

Experimental follow-up linked rosiglitazone to changes in resistance-associated expression and increased vincristine sensitivity in resistant cell models. The experiments connect a computational prediction to a measured drug response; the effects remain specific to the tested cell models and conditions.

2025 · Cancers

Adaptation to HSP90β inhibition

Cell models resistant to selective HSP90β inhibition showed changes in cytoskeletal, metabolic, and stress-response programs. We tested a combination with carboplatin that showed synergy in the resistant models.

Accepted manuscript · 2026

Purine metabolism in diffuse midline glioma

In H3K27M-mutant disease models, we linked Nuclear Factor I activity to altered purine metabolism. Targeting ATIC reduced tumor progression and improved survival in mice. The journal article is accepted; the link opens the earlier preprint.
