Resources / Software

Tools for exploring and communicating research

Use the miRNA Predictor to estimate mature microRNA abundance, Figure Tool to prepare editable scientific figures, and the HSP90 Atlas to explore published evidence about HSP90 isoforms. All three resources are available now.

01

Browser app
Browser edition available

miRNA Predictor

Estimate the abundance of 465 mature microRNAs from supported RNA-seq gene-expression tables. Review input coverage, inspect sample-level predictions and per-miRNA study comparisons, and download the results. Estimates support hypothesis generation and do not replace direct small-RNA measurements.

The standalone browser app runs on your device without sending expression files to an inference server.

Figure Tool

Create editable scientific figures and slide decks from plots, images, text, and vector artwork. Save portable project files and export SVG, PNG, or PDF.

Work is stored in the current browser; download project files to keep portable copies or move between devices.
